How to use

Always read and follow the directions on the product label first.

Shake well

Shake the bottle thoroughly before each use.

Spray the right spots

Target corners, cracks, wall joints and the paths pests use.

Keep food away

Cover or remove food and utensils before spraying.

Ventilate

Open doors and windows after spraying. Keep children and pets away from sprayed areas.

Keep out of reach of children. If the product contacts eyes or skin, rinse with clean water and seek medical advice if needed.
